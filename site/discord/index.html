A butler on Discord

Discord is a chat app for your phone and your computer. Your butler, called Alfred until you give it another name, is Koloft's own AI: one Claude, Codex, Muse or Antigravity session on your Mac, first in the sessions list. Give it a Discord channel (a chat room) and you can write to it from your phone too. It reaches your sessions for you: it starts them, reads them, sends them messages, and answers their questions the way you tell it to. Your other sessions stay off Discord until you want to work on one from your phone: then your butler opens a room just for that session. One exception: while you are away, a session with no room that needs a password, a code or a sign-in sends a 🔑 card to your butler's channel (see below).

Nothing runs on a server of ours, and you make no account with us. The words travel through your own private bot. A bot is an account that a program uses, not a person. You make it on Discord, and Koloft signs in as it. Until Koloft has the bot's token, nothing connects.

Set it up

Open Settings ▸ Discord and click Set up with Alfred (the button carries your butler's name), or, with Assist on, type "set up Discord" in ⌘P. Koloft starts a helper of your butler. It runs on your butler's agent. If that agent is switched off in Settings ▸ Sessions, it runs on your default agent instead. The helper opens the Discord website in its browser and does each step it can; you sign in, type your password and pass the "I am human" checks. Already have a bot? It can use it too. The steps are:

  1. Make the app, on the Discord website. An app is Discord's box that holds a bot. Or pick the app you already have.
  2. Get the bot's token. The token is the bot's password. Koloft reads it from the page itself, so it never passes through the AI, and keeps it in the macOS Keychain (the Mac's locked box for passwords). Once the token is in, Koloft turns on Message Content (Discord's switch that lets a bot read what you type).
  3. Make a private server, if you have none. A server is your own space in Discord, with channels in it. Only you are in this one.
  4. Add the bot to your server. The invite asks for the rights the bot needs, making channels among them.
  5. Make your butler's channel. Koloft makes #global in a category named after your bot, and ties it to your butler.

Koloft also needs to know who you are. If the bot's app is yours alone, Koloft already knows: it is the app's owner. If a team owns the app, send any message in your server, like "hi". It shows up in Settings ▸ Discord with the sender's name. Click This is me (or Not me). After that, Settings ▸ Discord shows your Discord account ("Only messages from this user reach Alfred."), the status ("The bot reads only the bound channel."), the server, and the channel. Click Set up with Alfred again any time to fix a problem: the steps that are done are skipped.

Its channel

Your butler has one channel. Under its name in the sessions list is that channel, or not on Discord. To pick one, right-click the butler's row ▸ Bind Discord channel…, or click Settings ▸ Discord ▸ Bind a channel…. The dialog lists your server's channels, with a new one on top that Koloft makes for you (#global, or #global-2 when that name is taken). Once it has one, the same menu has Change Discord channel… and Unbind Discord (only Discord goes; the butler and its conversation stay).

While your butler has a channel, it opens as soon as Koloft connects to Discord, and it never goes to sleep the way an idle session does.

Coming from an older Koloft, where each folder could have its own channel? The Global channel, or else the one you wrote in last, becomes your butler's. The other channels are left as they were, and no longer read. The session threads an older Koloft left under your butler's channel are archived once (the rooms below are new, and are left alone). Write in an old thread and Koloft answers Koloft no longer reads this thread. Talk to Alfred in #global.

From your phone

Talk to your butler

Write in the channel. Your message reaches your butler with "[Discord]" in front. It gets a ⏳ while it waits its turn, and a ✅ once it is in. If the butler is not open, your first message starts it; the ⏳ stays until it is ready. Files you attach are saved on your Mac for 7 days, and the butler is told where they are. Its answer to what you wrote comes back to the channel, and while it works on it the channel shows the bot typing.

When your butler asks you something, its card reads ❓ Alfred is waiting for you., with a button per option when the question has options. Once the question is answered, by a button, a typed reply or at the Mac, the card loses its buttons and says how it ended, like ✅ Answered: 2, ✅ Answered on the computer. or ⏹ The session closed. A button counts only once the session took the answer. If it did not, the card keeps its buttons, and only you see Koloft sent your answer, but the session still waits for it.

While you use Koloft

While you use Koloft (you typed, clicked, scrolled or moved the mouse in Koloft's window in the last 5 minutes, and the screen is not locked), Discord stays quiet: you see your butler in Koloft. Your butler does not post its replies to things you did not send from Discord, and the bot does not type. A question it asks on its own waits, and comes to the channel when you leave (the screen locks, or 5 minutes go by with no input in Koloft) if it is still open. After the screen locks, you count as away until you use Koloft again. Write from Discord and the answer still comes back there, questions included. If a question was waiting when you write, its card comes first, and Koloft tells you your message was not delivered: answer the question, then send your message again. A note that your butler could not reach a session shows in Koloft, not in Discord. A session that needs a password or a code asks in Koloft, and on Discord only if you are talking to it from there (see below).

While the moon in the sidebar is on, Discord stays quiet the same way. Any message, button press, form or slash command you send in your butler's channel turns the moon off.

Your sessions

A session in your folders does not post to Discord until it has a room. When your butler hands a session work, Koloft hands the session's reply back to the butler once its turn ends, and the butler tells you. A question from a session with no room shows only at the Mac: ask your butler what it is waiting on, and tell it what to pick or what to say. It reads the session's screen and answers, or presses the keys, as you would at the Mac. See what your butler can do.

While you are away, Koloft tells your butler about your sessions, on your Mac and on remote folders. When one finishes its turn, waits for an answer or an OK, or stops by surprise, Koloft waits at least half a minute to gather what else happens, then sends it all in one quiet message: which session, its folder, and, for a finished turn, the start of its reply. Your butler decides whether you need to know, and writes to you in the channel only if you do. Its answer to that message is not posted, and the bot does not type. Left out: your butler's own helpers, a turn whose reply already went back to whoever started the session, and a turn that only reports (see red dots). While the moon is on, no such message goes out; when you are back, your butler gets one note about the night instead, if anything happened.

A helper your butler started in its own folder is different: its questions come to the channel, titled with its name and (Alfred's helper). Like the butler's own, they wait while you use Koloft. Press a button on the card to answer one. Words you type in the channel go to your butler alone, never to a helper.

A session's own room

Want to go on with one session from your phone? Tell your butler, like "I'll take the login fix". It opens that session's room and answers with its link. A room is a thread (a side chat) under your butler's channel, named after the session. A session your butler starts while it answers you on Discord gets its room at once. The room opens with a 📍 card: where the session is, whether it is working, and the last thing it said.

  • What you write in the room goes to that session alone, and its reply comes back there. Words in your butler's channel still go to your butler alone.
  • Its questions come to the room as cards, with a button per option, like your butler's. A question Koloft cannot read says Koloft cannot read this question from here. Ask Alfred in #global to press its keys for you.
  • The rule for while you use Koloft holds here too: a reply to something you did not send from Discord is not posted, and a question waits until you leave.
  • /run, /clear and /compact typed in the room go to that session.
  • Press a key in the session's terminal at the Mac, and the room folds away (Discord archives it). It comes back when you ask your butler again or write in it.
  • It keeps the same room after /clear, a resume, a restart, or a restart of Koloft. After /clear the room says 🧹 Started over: it is a new conversation now, in this same room.
  • When the session closes, the room says ⏹ Closed. and folds away. Write in it and the session starts again and gets your words. If the session stops by surprise, the room says ⚠ It stopped unexpectedly.
  • A helper your butler started in its own folder gets no room: its questions, and what it shows with koloft open or koloft diff, come to your butler's channel.

While you are away, or talking to the session from its room, what it shows you with koloft open or koloft diff comes to the room too: a web page as pictures (at most 10, top to bottom), a Markdown file as text with the file attached, any other file as itself, and a diff as a picture of the changed lines. A file can be up to 20 MB (megabytes). A page on a remote folder's machine (an HTML file there, or its localhost) comes as pictures too, as that machine shows it.

What it shows while you are at the Mac is kept back. If it then asks you something in that same turn, and the card goes to Discord because you left, what it showed comes first, then the card. If the turn ends with no such card, it is never sent. A page is pictured as it looks when the card goes out.

Rooms work for every agent, on your Mac and in a remote folder.

Passwords and codes

Some things only you know, and they should not sit in a chat. When a session needs one, it asks in its own tab in Koloft (see how the agent asks). While you are away, or talking to that session from Discord, a 🔑 card comes too: to the session's room if it has one, or else to your butler's channel. It is headed with the session's name and its folder:

  • 🔑 fix-login · tidewatch asks for your sudo password (the Mac's admin password), with the command it wants to run
  • 🔑 <session> · <folder> asks you to fill in a field on a web page, with the page and the field
  • 🔑 <session> · <folder> needs the address a sign-in ends on, with the sign-in address to open on your phone

Press Enter it and Discord opens a form. What you type goes to Koloft, which passes it straight on: to sudo, into the page's field (and submits the form), or to the session's machine, which opens the address. The session never sees it, and it does not stay in the chat. The card then ends with ✅ Passed on., or ⌛ Koloft stopped waiting. if you did not answer in time, or ⏹ The session closed. The first answer wins, on the card or in Koloft. If you answer at the Mac, the card says ✅ Answered in Koloft. and loses its button; if you press Cancel there, it says ⏹ Turned down in Koloft.

While the moon is on, no 🔑 card is sent: Koloft turns the ask down and tells the session to skip that step.

Slash commands

A slash command is a command you type that starts with /. Discord shows three once your butler has a channel. Each goes to your butler, or, used in a session's room, to that session:

  • /run — type a command into your butler, like /context or /model haiku for a butler on Claude; each agent has its own commands
  • /clear — start a new conversation with your butler
  • /compact — shrink your butler's conversation; you may say what to keep

A plain message that starts with /, with no file attached, is typed into your butler the same way. A command waits until the butler's turn ends, for up to 10 minutes. What it printed comes back to the channel. While your butler's own question card waits, a command is not typed: Koloft asks you to answer the question first.

Files and pictures out

Your butler can send you files. It runs koloft discord send <file>... -- "<text>" with each file's full path, and the text part may be left out. Each file can be up to 20 MB (megabytes). Discord takes 10 files and 25 MB per message, so a bigger batch goes out as several messages.

It can also send you a picture of a page with koloft discord shot <HTML file or web address>. Koloft opens the page out of sight and takes a picture of the whole page; a page taller than one picture comes as several, top to bottom, at most 10.

What your butler, or a helper in its folder, shows you with koloft open or koloft diff comes to its channel too, the same way a session's comes to its room.

While you use Koloft, your butler cannot send files or pictures with koloft discord send or shot, unless it is answering what you sent from Discord. It is told to show the file or page in the Workbench instead.

A message you send in your butler's channel while Koloft is closed gets one answer: "Koloft was offline, this message was not delivered." One you send in a session's room waits, and goes to that session, in order, once Koloft is back.

What Discord sees

Everything goes to Discord, at discord.com/api/v10 and gateway.discord.gg, and files you attach come down from Discord's file server. This is what leaves your Mac:

what what is in it
butler replies Every reply your butler writes, whole, and its own question cards; while you use Koloft, only its answers to what you sent from Discord. Never its answer to Koloft's quiet message about your sessions.
rooms For a session you gave a room: its name, folder and agent, its replies and its question cards (held back while you use Koloft, as your butler's are), and copies of what it shows with koloft open or koloft diff: at once while you are away or talking to it from there, or, if shown while you use Koloft, just before a question card it sends in that same turn.
helper questions The question cards of the helpers your butler started in its own folder, held while you use Koloft like the butler's, and copies of what they show with koloft open or koloft diff.
files and pictures What your butler sends with koloft discord send or shot; while you use Koloft, only when it answers what you sent from Discord.
🔑 cards The session's name and folder, and what it asks for (the command for sudo, the page and field, or the sign-in address), only while you are away or talking to that session from Discord. Never what you type into the form.
command results What /run, /clear or /compact printed in your butler, or in a session through its room.
typing While your butler works on what you sent, or while you are away, a typing signal every 8 seconds; none while it reads Koloft's quiet message about your sessions.
errors A short note when something goes wrong; while you use Koloft, a note that your butler could not reach a session shows in Koloft instead.

A long reply is split into several messages.

  • Only your account, the one Settings ▸ Discord shows, is heard. Other people and bots are ignored, and their commands and button presses refused.
  • Discord still sends every message in the server to your Mac. Koloft drops the rest there, and none of it goes back out.
  • If a Discord team owns the bot's app, then until you pick yourself, the last message anyone wrote in the server shows up in Settings ▸ Discord, so you can say whether it is you.
  • The token signs the bot in before you bind a channel. Until you bind one, it ignores messages and shows no slash commands.

What it won't do

  • post your whole transcript (no session's transcript, the written record of a session: only what your butler and the sessions with a room write, and the cards above)
  • post your sessions' news (a session without a room posts nothing; ask your butler how one is doing)
  • run two Kolofts on one data folder (the second one waits for the bot; a Koloft on another Mac with the same token is not stopped, and both get every message)
