01 · One line starts a session

Say what you want. Skip the where and how.

Press ⌘P and type the job. With Assist on, Koloft picks the folder, opens a worktree in a git repo and names the session; the real Claude Code or Codex starts on your words. The same box jumps to any session and searches everything you said.

notePicking the place needs Koloft Assist on (Settings ▸ Assist); with it off, ⏎ opens the folder picker with your words kept. ⏎ starts your default tool, ⇧⏎ the other; Codex sessions take their title from Codex.

Everything ⌘P does
02 · Sessions that start sessions

Your agent splits the work. You see every piece.

An agent runs koloft session new and a real session opens under the one that started it, in a worktree of its own when it asks for one. Click into any of them and keep talking. When a child finishes its first turn, its result goes back to the parent.

Real sessions, not hidden sub-tasksEach child has its own tab, terminal and Workbench. Watch it work, answer it, or take it over.
A tree you can foldChildren hang under the session that started them; a folded parent shows how many inside need you.
They talk to each otherkoloft session send passes a message between sessions; the answer comes back when that turn ends.

noteWorks for Claude Code and Codex. In a remote folder the child opens on that machine, beside its parent.

What agents can do with Koloft
03 · A workbench for every session

The agent keeps its terminal. It gets a browser too.

The middle is Claude Code's or Codex's own terminal; Koloft draws nothing over it. Beside it hangs that session's Workbench: a real browser the agent can open and click, a terminal tab in the session's folder, and every change as a live diff.

A browser you share with the agentIt opens pages with the playwright-cli that ships inside, clicks and reads them; sites you signed into in the Workbench stay signed in for it.
A terminal tab beside it⌃` opens a shell in the session's folder: run the app, the tests, git, without leaving the session.
Every change, liveEach file it touches shows up as a diff; ✎ on a hunk drops your note into its input box.

noteIn remote folders the terminal tab and changes work; the agent can't drive the browser there yet.

Everything in the Workbench
04 · Talk to Koloft

Say it in plain words. Assist runs Koloft for you.

Assist is Koloft's own helper. Tell it what you want, here or on your phone: it starts, resumes, stops and closes sessions, reads what they said, answers their questions, presses their keys, runs slash commands in them and sets up scheduled jobs. It runs your sessions; it is not there to edit your files.

From your phoneBind Assist to your own Discord bot: one thread per session, Yes / No buttons for its questions, files both ways. Assist sets the bot up for you.
It answers and presses keysA session stuck on a question or a menu? Tell Assist “pick the second one”: it reads the screen and presses the keys.
It sets up schedules“Run the tests every night at 2 and write a short report” becomes a scheduled job for that folder.

noteTurn Assist on in Settings ▸ Assist. It runs on your Mac; it can message a session in a remote folder, not start one there. It can't change Settings for you.

Everything Assist does
05 · Agents that keep working

Work keeps going while you're away.

Put a job on a schedule and Koloft starts a fresh session for it every hour, day or week; in a git repo, in a worktree of its own. Read what it did in the morning, here or by asking Assist from your phone.

On a schedule

Hourly, daily or on the days you pick, as a sentence or a /skill. Tell it to close when done and it tidies up, unless work is left uncommitted or unmerged.

Keep running

Pin a session and it never sleeps, and it starts in the background every time Koloft opens.

On another machine alpha

In a folder you reach over SSH, Claude runs in tmux, so it keeps going when the line drops or your Mac sleeps.

noteSchedules on your Mac run while it is awake: turn on Koloft ▸ Keep Mac Awake, or use a remote folder.

Several accounts, one pool

A new session starts on the Claude or Codex account with the most room; no more “limit reached”.

Pull requests and checks

Commit, push and open the PR from the Workbench; a failing check's log goes back to the session in one click.

Everything comes with it

git, gh and playwright-cli ship inside; one click installs Claude Code or Codex.

Our promise

What it will never do

  • ask you to sign up

    No Koloft account. It runs on your own Claude or Codex login.

  • track you

    No analytics. The few places it talks to are all listed.

  • copy your chats

    It reads the tools' own records; a remote folder's are mirrored to your Mac, and nothing goes anywhere else.

  • cover the agent

    Nothing is drawn over the agent's terminal.

Give your agents a home.
