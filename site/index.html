Not a replacement. Koloft runs the claude and codex you already have and never covers their screen. A terminal can't tell you which of nine tabs is waiting, can't spread your sessions over two accounts, and can't hand out work for you.

01 · ⌘P, one box for everything

Type a line. It starts, finds or searches.

With Assist on, one sentence becomes a working session: it picks the folder, a worktree and a title. A few letters jump to any session. ⌥⏎ searches every conversation on this Mac.

Assist picks the placeThe folder, a worktree of its own when the folder is a git repo, and a short title. Not sure? It asks you which folder.
Start from an issue⇧⌘N lists a GitHub repo's open issues and pull requests; pick one and it starts in its own worktree, told to read it first.
Ask Koloft itselfType “set up Discord” and Assist takes it as a job, so you skip the Settings pages.

notePicking the place and asking Koloft itself need Koloft Assist on (Settings ▸ Assist); with it off, ⏎ opens the folder picker with your words kept. Codex runs in local folders only.

Everything ⌘P does
02 · Every session, one list

Ten sessions, five folders. You still know which is waiting.

Read from the tools' own files, nothing to import. A red dot when a turn ends; ⌘J jumps to the one that has waited longest.

Lights that mean somethingBreathing orange: working. Green with a red dot: done, not seen yet. Amber: waiting for your OK.
Sessions an agent startsThey hang under the session that started them, as a tree you can fold; a folded parent shows how many need you.
Sleep, then wake as they wereA quiet session's program goes to sleep; its row, tab, screen and Workbench stay, and a click wakes it. Quit and reopen Koloft: everything comes back.
Everything about sessions
03 · The real terminal, and a Workbench

The agent keeps its screen. Everything else hangs beside it.

The middle is Claude Code's or Codex's own terminal; Koloft draws nothing over it. Beside it, for that one session: every change as a live diff, the files, a real browser, a shell.

Everything in the Workbench
Comment on a change✎ on any hunk; the file, the hunk and your note land in the session's input box for you to send.
A real browser, not a previewWeb tabs keep your logins, downloads and pop-ups, one set per session (on your Mac; not yet in remote folders).
Pull requests and checksThe GitHub button shows the PR and its checks. Commit, push and open the PR there; a failing check's log goes back to the session in one click.
04 · Agents use Koloft too

Your agents get the same tools you do.

Every session Koloft starts gets a koloft command and a real browser. The agent opens its work beside you, drives the web as you, and hands jobs to a sibling.

It drives your browser

With the playwright-cli that ships inside, the agent opens pages in its own Workbench tabs, clicks, fills and reads them, signed in as you.

open lands beside it

When the agent runs open report.md or open localhost:3000, it shows in that session's Workbench, not a new window.

It hands work to a sibling

koloft session new -w tests starts a session in its own worktree; when it is done, its reply comes back to the one that asked.

noteIn remote folders the agent can't drive the browser yet, and a bare open is not caught; koloft open still shows a file in the Workbench.

What agents can do with Koloft
05 · Assist hands out the work

Hand it the work. It reports back.

Koloft's own helper starts sessions, answers their questions and tells you when a turn ends, here or from your own Discord bot on your phone. It is there to pass work on, not to edit your files.

From your phoneBind Assist to your own Discord bot: one thread per session, Yes / No buttons for its questions, files both ways.
It sets Discord up for youOne click: Assist does the clicking and typing on Discord's site; you sign in, pass the human checks, and press the few buttons only a person may.
It presses keys for youA session stuck on a menu? Tell Assist “pick the second one”: it reads the screen and presses the keys.

noteTurn Assist on in Settings ▸ Assist. It runs on your Mac and starts sessions there; it can message a session in a remote folder once that session's turn ends. Questions with several parts, pick-many answers or free text get no Discord buttons: tell Assist what to pick.

Everything Assist does
06 · Several accounts, one pool

No more “limit reached”.

Add your Claude and Codex accounts. A new session starts on the one with the most room; a resumed one stays put until it is 95% used.

noteshown here: three Claude accounts. Codex accounts sit in the same list with their own bars.

Accounts and usage
Usage at a glanceA capsule in the top bar: hover for each account's 5-hour and 7-day use and when it resets; Codex accounts show their balance and saved resets.
Git accounts, one per repositoryGitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Azure DevOps, Gitea or any server; every push and gh in that folder uses the one you picked.

Sessions on a timer

Every hour, day or week; in a git repo, in a worktree of its own. Tell it to close when done and it tidies up, unless work is left uncommitted or unmerged.

Folders on another machine alpha

A folder you reach over SSH. Claude runs there in tmux, so a dropped line doesn't stop it. Claude only for now.

Everything comes with it

git, gh, playwright-cli and a Nerd Font ship inside. On a fresh Mac, one click installs Claude Code or Codex.

Our promise

What it will never do

  • ask you to sign up

    No Koloft account. It runs on your own Claude or Codex login.

  • track you

    No analytics. The few places it talks to are all listed.

  • copy your chats

    It reads the tools' own records; a remote folder's are mirrored to your Mac, and nothing goes anywhere else.

  • cover the agent

    Nothing is drawn over the agent's terminal.
