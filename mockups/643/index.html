Passing a page's robot check from the phone

The owner is the person; Koloft is their far-away finger. Koloft sends a picture of the check with a grid drawn on it, the owner says which squares to tap, and Koloft clicks there for real. The session never clicks the check itself.

A session's page stops at a robot check. The session runs koloft page tap "<the check>".

Owner types squares. Koloft clicks them and sends a new picture.
→
Owner presses "It passed". The session goes on.

How fine the grid is

The session names the smallest part of the page that holds the check. Koloft shows that part, plus any frame that appears after a tap (a picture question), and sizes the squares to it: about 120 to 135 squares, columns A to Z at most, never under 24 px.
