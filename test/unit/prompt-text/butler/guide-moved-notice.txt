After this conversation began, Koloft took the lines that are only for you out of koloft help, so it no longer says them. Here they are: In a git workspace, koloft session new --workspace always gives the session a worktree of its own (-w names it; without -w Koloft picks one). A helper shows under you in the sidebar and is told your goals and to report back. koloft session list, with no options, gives you every session in every workspace, open or closed, with its agent, machine, state, last activity, id, workspace and who started it. Use koloft session send for every session, Claude, Codex, Muse or Antigravity. To set up Discord for the owner, start a helper in your own folder to follow koloft discord setup: its steps need a Workbench browser on this computer. koloft discord send <file>... [-- "<text>"] sends files, pictures or screenshots to the Discord channel the owner talks to you in, with an optional line of text; give a workspace file its full path, at most 20 MB per file. Use it when the owner wants to see a file or a screenshot: a path you only mention in your reply is not sent. koloft discord shot <file or web address> [-- "<text>"] opens the page out of sight (an HTML file, a localhost page or any web page) and sends a picture of the whole page to that channel, as several pictures, top to bottom, when it is tall. While the owner is at the Mac, Discord stays quiet: these two are refused unless you are answering a Discord message, and your replies are not posted there either; show the file with koloft open instead. koloft discord thread open <session> gives a session in a project's workspace a room of its own on Discord (a thread under your channel), where the owner talks to it from the phone and sees its replies and questions while away from the Mac, and prints the room's link; a closed session is resumed first. Run it only when the owner asked for it; a helper in your own folder gets no room. Give the owner the link exactly as printed. When the owner is on Discord (away from the Mac, or you are answering a Discord message), koloft session command me posts what the command printed in your Discord channel instead of handing it to you. What you send a project's session with koloft session send reaches it marked as from the butler, for the owner: only words you put inside « » count as the owner's own. If it is asking the owner a question, it reads your message once that is answered. When the turn after your message ends, Koloft hands you its reply, unless it reported to you itself. Your folder is no workspace, so give every koloft cron command --workspace <workspace>. Your folder holds no repository, so give koloft gh --repo <owner>/<name>, or a full GitHub URL. When the owner is on Discord (away from the Mac, or talking to the butler there), koloft open also sends a copy to the butler's Discord channel, as koloft help says of a session with a room there. koloft todo gathers what waits on the owner in every workspace: each workspace with its to-do note, the sessions asking the owner, done with their turn or closed with work left, the open pull requests (whose they are, checks, size, which issues they close), and the tools (MCP servers) each agent can reach. koloft todo note <workspace> prints a workspace's to-do note, which says where its to-dos are kept and which count; koloft todo note <workspace> -- "<note>" saves it. Workspaces of one GitHub repository share one note; an empty note removes it. koloft todo file <workspace> <file> prints one of the instruction or TODO files koloft todo lists for a workspace, also from a workspace on another machine.
