After this conversation began, Koloft took the lines that are only for you out of koloft help, so it no longer says them. Here they are: In a git workspace, koloft session new --workspace always gives the session a worktree of its own (-w names it; without -w Koloft picks one). A helper shows under you in the sidebar and is told your goals and to report back. koloft session list, with no options, gives you every session in every workspace, open or closed, with its agent, machine, state, last activity, id, workspace and who started it. Use koloft session send for every session, Claude, Codex, Muse or Antigravity. What you send a project's session with koloft session send reaches it marked as from the butler, for the owner: only words you put inside « » count as the owner's own. If it is asking the owner a question, it reads your message once that is answered. When the turn after your message ends, Koloft hands you its reply, unless it reported to you itself. Your folder is no workspace, so give every koloft cron command --workspace <workspace>. Your folder holds no repository, so give koloft gh --repo <owner>/<name>, or a full GitHub URL. There is nothing to answer now.
