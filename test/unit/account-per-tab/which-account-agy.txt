=== Antigravity ===
a new session, two subscriptions and a key                             → made-up-second
resuming a session that ran on the busier subscription                 → made-up-first
resuming a session whose subscription is at its limit                  → made-up-second
every subscription at its limit, a key account there                   → made-up-key
every subscription at its limit, nothing else (the first back)         → made-up-first
only a key account                                                     → made-up-key
the only subscription disabled, the other expired                      → none
no account at all                                                      → none
only other agents' kinds (oauth, apikey, custom, codex-home)           → none
only asking twice, then two starts, two accounts with equal room       → asked made-up-left, made-up-left; started made-up-left, made-up-right
